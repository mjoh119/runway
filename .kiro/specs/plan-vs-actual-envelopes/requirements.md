# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This is Spec 2 of the Runway app: plan versus actual, and envelopes. Spec 1 delivered the projection engine and the Cycle_View, which show the runway computed from planned entries. This spec lets the user record what actually happened (post an actual, skip an entry, add a one-off), see how actuals differ from estimates (variance), and track flexible spending in envelopes. The runway recomputes immediately after every change.

A one-off can describe something that already happened or something still to come. The Add one-off form has a "This already happened" option: a one-off marked as already happened is saved directly as POSTED and updates the balances at once, while a planned one-off must be dated today or later. A planned entry becomes overdue only when its date passes without being posted or skipped.

Because the demo has no bank feed, the app itself keeps the Primary_Account balances current when an actual is posted, an already-happened one-off is saved, or a purchase is logged. The Projection_Engine then excludes posted entries, as Spec 1 requires, so posting a bill at its estimate does not move the runway.

Out of scope for this spec: transfers, schedule and account editing, Plaid, envelope deletion, and un-posting or editing a posted actual.

Existing conventions this spec relies on (from Spec 1):

- Money is integer cents. Entry amounts are stored signed, with debits negative and income positive. Users type positive amounts and the app applies the sign from the entry direction.
- Dates are date-only `YYYY-MM-DD` strings.
- The projection treats a Posted entry as already included in the account balances, and treats a Skipped entry as absent.
- The projection recomputes shifted dates for one-off entries, so Envelope_Commitment entries need an unshifted date (see Requirement 9).

## Glossary

- **Runway_App**: the application, comprising the service layer, the Projection_Engine, and the user interface.
- **Projection_Engine**: the pure-function module in `src/lib/projection/` defined in Spec 1. It performs no I/O.
- **Entry_Service**: the service layer that validates and persists entry mutations (post, skip, add one-off, log purchase) and balance updates, always scoped to the current Workspace.
- **Envelope_Calculator**: the pure-function module in `src/lib/envelopes/` that computes envelope periods, spent, left, overage, and Envelope_Commitments. It performs no I/O.
- **Money_Parser**: the pure function that converts user-typed money text into integer cents.
- **Cycle_View**: the hero screen at `/` defined in Spec 1.
- **Envelopes_Screen**: the screen at `/envelopes` that lists envelopes and their line items.
- **Log_Flow**: the fast entry flow with three modes: post an actual, add a purchase, and add a one-off. It is reachable from the Cycle_View and the Envelopes_Screen.
- **Settings_Screen**: the screen at `/settings` that edits the Low_Balance_Threshold and the Projection_Horizon.
- **Workspace**: an isolated data scope identified by a workspace id, as defined in Spec 1. Every persisted record carries `workspaceId`.
- **Primary_Account**: the single account flagged as the user's main spending account, as defined in Spec 1.
- **Entry**: one dated row with an estimated amount and an optional actual amount, as defined in Spec 1.
- **Entry_Status**: one of PLANNED, PENDING, POSTED, or SKIPPED, as defined in Spec 1.
- **Schedule_Occurrence**: one entry generated by a Schedule for a specific original post date.
- **Occurrence_Record**: a persisted Entry that represents one Schedule_Occurrence the user has posted or skipped. It carries the Schedule id and the occurrence's original post date.
- **Scheduled_Income**: an income Entry that originates from a Schedule, whether still planned or already posted through an Occurrence_Record. A one-off income Entry is not Scheduled_Income.
- **Actual_Date**: the calendar date the user records as the date an actual amount settled.
- **Variance**: for one Entry, the signed actual amount minus the signed estimated amount, in integer cents. A negative Variance means less cash than planned (a debit larger than estimated or an income smaller than estimated). A positive Variance means more cash than planned.
- **Cumulative_Variance**: the sum of Variance over all Posted entries whose Actual_Date falls inside the current Pay_Cycle.
- **Envelope**: a flexible spending category with a name, a budget in integer cents, and a Reset_Period, scoped to a Workspace.
- **Reset_Period**: one of EACH_PAY_CYCLE, WEEKLY, or MONTHLY. This choice is made by the user per Envelope.
- **Envelope_Period**: the date range [start, end] of an Envelope's Reset_Period that contains a given date. A WEEKLY period runs Monday through Sunday. A MONTHLY period runs from the 1st through the last day of a calendar month. An EACH_PAY_CYCLE period runs from the shifted date of the latest Scheduled_Income on or before the given date through the day before the next Scheduled_Income, and starts at the Current_Date when no Scheduled_Income exists on or before the Current_Date.
- **Current_Period**: the Envelope_Period that contains the Current_Date.
- **Line_Item**: a purchase assigned to exactly one Envelope, with an amount in integer cents, a purchase date, and an optional description.
- **Spent**: the sum of Line_Item amounts in an Envelope whose purchase date falls inside the Current_Period.
- **Left**: the budget minus Spent, floored at 0.
- **Overage**: the amount by which Spent exceeds the budget, or 0 when Spent does not exceed the budget.
- **Envelope_Commitment**: a synthetic, non-persisted debit Entry equal to Left, supplied to the Projection_Engine for each Envelope whose Left is greater than 0.
- **Commitment_Date**: the later of the Current_Period start date and the Current_Date.
- **Submission_Key**: a client-generated unique identifier sent with each create or post request and stored per Workspace, used to detect repeat submissions.
- **Current_Date**: the visitor's local calendar date from the browser, as defined in Spec 1.
- **Overdue_Entry**: an Entry with status PLANNED whose shifted post date has passed with no actual amount recorded and which has been neither posted nor skipped, as defined in Spec 1. An Entry with status POSTED or SKIPPED is never an Overdue_Entry.
- **Already_Happened_Option**: the "This already happened" checkbox on the add-one-off mode of the Log_Flow. When checked, the one-off is saved as POSTED. When unchecked, the one-off is saved as PLANNED.
- **Posted_One_Off**: a one-off Entry saved with the Already_Happened_Option checked, carrying status POSTED, an actual amount equal to its estimated amount, and an Actual_Date equal to the entered date.
- **Hold_Release_Date**: the date a deposit becomes available, as defined in Spec 1.
- **Low_Balance_Threshold**: the user-set amount in integer cents, as defined in Spec 1.
- **Projection_Horizon**: the number of days the projection runs when no future income is scheduled, as defined in Spec 1.
- **Demo_Seed**: the fictional-persona data written into a Workspace on first visit and on "Reset demo".

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Money Input Parsing

**User Story:** As a user, I want typed dollar amounts converted to exact integer cents, so that the amounts I enter are never rounded or corrupted.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Money_Parser receives text consisting of digits with an optional decimal point followed by one or two digits, THE Money_Parser SHALL return the integer number of cents (for example "12.5" returns 1250, "12" returns 1200, "12.05" returns 1205, and "0.99" returns 99).
2. WHEN the Money_Parser receives text with leading or trailing whitespace around an otherwise valid amount, THE Money_Parser SHALL ignore the whitespace and return the integer cents.
3. IF the Money_Parser receives text with more than two digits after the decimal point, THEN THE Money_Parser SHALL return an error stating that at most two decimal places are allowed, and SHALL NOT round the value.
4. IF the Money_Parser receives empty text or whitespace-only text, THEN THE Money_Parser SHALL return an error stating that an amount is required.
5. IF the Money_Parser receives text that is not a plain non-negative decimal number (for example "abc", "1e3", "-5", "1,200", "$5", "5.", or ".5"), THEN THE Money_Parser SHALL return an error stating that the amount is not a valid number.
6. IF the Money_Parser receives an amount of 0 cents, or an amount greater than 999,999,999 cents, THEN THE Money_Parser SHALL return an error stating the allowed range of 1 to 999,999,999 cents.
7. FOR ALL integer cent values from 1 to 999,999,999, formatting the value as a dollars-and-cents string with two decimals and then parsing that string SHALL return the original value (round-trip property).
8. THE Runway_App SHALL compute, store, transmit, and display every amount introduced by this spec using integer arithmetic, with no floating-point operations on money.
9. IF the Money_Parser returns an error, THEN THE Log_Flow, the Envelopes_Screen, and the Settings_Screen SHALL display the error message next to the offending field, and THE Runway_App SHALL NOT change any stored data.

### Requirement 2: Post an Actual Against a Planned Entry

**User Story:** As a user, I want to record the actual amount and date of a planned entry, so that the app reflects what really happened and the runway stays accurate.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user submits an actual amount and an Actual_Date for an Entry whose status is PLANNED or PENDING, THE Entry_Service SHALL record the actual amount (signed to match the Entry's direction), record the Actual_Date, and set the Entry's status to POSTED.
2. WHEN the user opens the post-actual mode for an Entry, THE Log_Flow SHALL pre-fill the amount field with the Entry's estimated amount and the date field with the Current_Date.
3. IF the user submits an Actual_Date later than the Current_Date, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the post with an error stating that an actual cannot be dated in the future, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
4. IF the user attempts to post an Entry whose status is POSTED or SKIPPED, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the post with an error stating the Entry cannot be posted from its current status, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
5. WHEN the user posts a Schedule_Occurrence that has no Occurrence_Record, THE Entry_Service SHALL create an Occurrence_Record with status POSTED carrying the Schedule id, the occurrence's original post date, the Schedule's estimated amount, the actual amount, and the Actual_Date.
6. WHERE a Schedule_Occurrence has an Occurrence_Record, THE Projection_Engine SHALL NOT generate a planned entry for that occurrence, and SHALL use the Occurrence_Record in its place.
7. WHEN an Entry is posted with an actual amount greater than its estimate, THE Entry_Service SHALL store the actual amount unchanged and keep the estimated amount unchanged.
8. WHEN an Entry is posted with an actual amount less than its estimate, THE Entry_Service SHALL store the actual amount unchanged and keep the estimated amount unchanged.
9. WHEN an Overdue_Entry is posted, THE Runway_App SHALL remove that Entry from the overdue warning and remove its Overdue indicator from the Cycle_View timeline.
10. WHEN a Scheduled_Income Entry is posted, THE Runway_App SHALL keep that Entry as a Pay_Cycle boundary at its shifted post date.

### Requirement 3: Balance Update on Posting

**User Story:** As a user of a demo with no bank feed, I want my account balances to move when I post an actual, so that the projection starts from what I really have.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a debit Entry with status PLANNED is posted with actual amount A, THE Entry_Service SHALL decrease the ledger balance of the Entry's account by A and decrease the available balance by A, in the same transaction as the status change.
2. WHEN an income Entry with status PLANNED and no Hold_Release_Date later than the Actual_Date is posted with actual amount A, THE Entry_Service SHALL increase the ledger balance by A and increase the available balance by A.
3. WHEN an income Entry with a Hold_Release_Date later than the Actual_Date is posted with actual amount A, THE Entry_Service SHALL increase the ledger balance by A and leave the available balance unchanged.
4. WHEN a debit Entry with status PENDING and estimated amount E is posted with actual amount A, THE Entry_Service SHALL decrease the ledger balance by A and change the available balance by E minus A (because the pending amount E is already reflected in the available balance).
5. WHILE an account's available balance is null, THE Entry_Service SHALL leave the available balance null when updating balances.
6. IF any step of a post, skip, one-off creation, or purchase operation fails, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL roll back all changes made by that operation so that no Entry status or balance changes are persisted.
7. FOR ALL planned debit or income Entries without a Hold_Release_Date whose shifted post date is on or before the Current_Date, posting the Entry on the Current_Date at its estimated amount SHALL leave the projected daily available balance series and the runway lowest balance unchanged (invariant property).
8. THE Runway_App SHALL include an automated test proving that the runway lowest balance and runway date do not change when a planned bill whose shifted post date is the Current_Date is posted at its estimated amount.
9. WHEN a Posted_One_Off debit with amount A is saved, THE Entry_Service SHALL decrease the Primary_Account ledger balance by A and decrease the available balance by A, in the same transaction as the creation of the Entry.
10. WHEN a Posted_One_Off income with amount A and no Hold_Release_Date later than its Actual_Date is saved, THE Entry_Service SHALL increase the Primary_Account ledger balance by A and increase the available balance by A, in the same transaction as the creation of the Entry.
11. WHEN a Posted_One_Off income with amount A and a Hold_Release_Date later than its Actual_Date is saved, THE Entry_Service SHALL increase the Primary_Account ledger balance by A and leave the available balance unchanged, in the same transaction as the creation of the Entry.
12. WHILE the Primary_Account available balance is null, THE Entry_Service SHALL leave the available balance null when saving a Posted_One_Off.

### Requirement 4: Skip an Entry

**User Story:** As a user, I want to skip an entry that will not happen, so that the projection no longer counts it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user skips an Entry whose status is PLANNED or PENDING, THE Entry_Service SHALL set the Entry's status to SKIPPED without changing any account balance.
2. WHEN the user skips a Schedule_Occurrence that has no Occurrence_Record, THE Entry_Service SHALL create an Occurrence_Record with status SKIPPED carrying the Schedule id and the occurrence's original post date.
3. WHILE an Entry's status is SKIPPED, THE Projection_Engine SHALL exclude the Entry from balance projection, runway, Pay_Cycle boundaries, the overdue warning, and Cumulative_Variance.
4. IF the user attempts to skip an Entry whose status is POSTED or SKIPPED, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error stating the Entry cannot be skipped from its current status, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
5. WHEN an Overdue_Entry is skipped, THE Runway_App SHALL remove that Entry from the overdue warning.

### Requirement 5: Add a One-Off Entry

**User Story:** As a user, I want to add a one-time debit or income that is not part of any schedule, so that unexpected money in or out is reflected in my runway.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user submits a direction (debit or income), an amount, a date, an optional description, and the Already_Happened_Option unchecked, THE Entry_Service SHALL create a one-off Entry on the Primary_Account with status PLANNED, estimated amount equal to the amount (signed to match the direction), and no actual amount.
2. WHEN a PLANNED one-off Entry is created, THE Entry_Service SHALL leave all account balances unchanged.
3. IF the Already_Happened_Option is unchecked and the submitted date is earlier than the Current_Date, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with the error "Planned entries need a date today or later", and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
4. WHEN a one-off income Entry is created or posted, THE Projection_Engine SHALL treat it as a Pay_Cycle boundary, as Spec 1 requires, and THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL NOT treat it as an Envelope_Period boundary.
5. IF the submitted date is not a valid calendar date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error stating the date is invalid, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
6. IF the submitted direction is neither debit nor income, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error stating the direction is invalid, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
7. WHEN the user submits a direction, an amount, a date on or before the Current_Date, an optional description, and the Already_Happened_Option checked, THE Entry_Service SHALL create a Posted_One_Off on the Primary_Account with status POSTED, estimated amount equal to the amount (signed to match the direction), actual amount equal to the same signed amount, and Actual_Date equal to the submitted date.
8. WHEN a Posted_One_Off is created, THE Entry_Service SHALL update the Primary_Account ledger and available balances according to Requirement 3 criteria 9 to 12, in the same transaction as the creation of the Entry.
9. THE Runway_App SHALL NOT show a Posted_One_Off as an Overdue_Entry, whatever its date.
10. WHEN the submitted date is later than the Current_Date and the Already_Happened_Option is unchecked, THE Entry_Service SHALL create the one-off Entry with status PLANNED.
11. IF the Already_Happened_Option is checked and the submitted date is later than the Current_Date, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error stating that an actual cannot be dated in the future, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
12. WHEN a PLANNED one-off Entry whose date has passed has been neither posted nor skipped, THE Runway_App SHALL show it as an Overdue_Entry until it is posted or skipped.
13. THE Runway_App SHALL show an Entry as an Overdue_Entry only when its status is PLANNED.
14. THE Runway_App SHALL include an automated test proving that a one-off with a date earlier than the Current_Date and the Already_Happened_Option checked becomes POSTED with actual and estimated amounts equal to the entered amount, updates the Primary_Account ledger and available balances, and is not an Overdue_Entry.
15. THE Runway_App SHALL include an automated test proving that a planned one-off with a date earlier than the Current_Date is rejected with the exact message "Planned entries need a date today or later" and that no Entry, balance, or Submission_Key changes.

### Requirement 6: Variance

**User Story:** As a user, I want to see how each actual differs from its estimate and how far off I am this pay cycle, so that I know whether my plan is realistic.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Runway_App SHALL compute Variance for an Entry as the signed actual amount minus the signed estimated amount, in integer cents.
2. WHEN a debit Entry with estimate -8,500 is posted at -9,200, THE Runway_App SHALL report a Variance of -700.
3. WHEN a debit Entry with estimate -8,500 is posted at -7,000, THE Runway_App SHALL report a Variance of +1,500.
4. WHEN an income Entry with estimate 240,000 is posted at 235,000, THE Runway_App SHALL report a Variance of -5,000.
5. WHEN an Entry is posted at exactly its estimate, THE Runway_App SHALL report a Variance of 0.
6. THE Runway_App SHALL compute Cumulative_Variance as the sum of Variance over all POSTED Entries whose Actual_Date falls within the current Pay_Cycle, excluding SKIPPED Entries and excluding Line_Items.
7. THE Cycle_View SHALL display a list of POSTED Entries in the current Pay_Cycle showing, for each, its description, estimated amount, actual amount, and Variance.
8. THE Cycle_View SHALL display the Cumulative_Variance for the current Pay_Cycle, styled as unfavorable when negative, favorable when positive, and neutral when 0.
9. WHEN an Entry is posted, THE Cycle_View SHALL update the displayed per-entry Variance and Cumulative_Variance without a manual page reload.
10. FOR ALL sets of POSTED Entries, THE Runway_App SHALL compute Cumulative_Variance equal to the sum of per-entry Variance values (invariant property).
11. WHEN a Posted_One_Off has an Actual_Date within the current Pay_Cycle, THE Runway_App SHALL report a Variance of 0 for it, include it in the Cumulative_Variance sum, and include it in the Cycle_View list of POSTED Entries.

### Requirement 7: Envelope Setup

**User Story:** As a user, I want to create envelopes with a budget and a reset period I choose, so that my flexible spending has a plan.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user submits a name, a budget amount, and a Reset_Period of EACH_PAY_CYCLE, WEEKLY, or MONTHLY, THE Entry_Service SHALL create an Envelope in the current Workspace.
2. WHEN the user edits an Envelope's name, budget, or Reset_Period, THE Entry_Service SHALL save the change and THE Runway_App SHALL recompute the Envelope's Spent, Left, and Overage and the runway.
3. IF the submitted name is empty after trimming whitespace, or longer than 60 characters, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error describing the name rule, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
4. IF the submitted name matches another Envelope in the same Workspace, ignoring letter case, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error stating the name is already in use, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
5. IF the submitted budget fails the Money_Parser rules, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with the Money_Parser error, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
6. IF the submitted Reset_Period is not one of EACH_PAY_CYCLE, WEEKLY, or MONTHLY, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error stating the period is invalid, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
7. THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL compute the Envelope_Period for a WEEKLY Envelope as Monday through Sunday containing the given date.
8. THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL compute the Envelope_Period for a MONTHLY Envelope as the first through last day of the calendar month containing the given date, including February in leap years.
9. THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL compute the Envelope_Period for an EACH_PAY_CYCLE Envelope using only Scheduled_Income boundaries, so that a one-off income Entry does not start or end the period.
10. WHEN a Scheduled_Income occurrence is skipped, THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL NOT use that occurrence as an Envelope_Period boundary.

### Requirement 8: Line Items and Envelope Totals

**User Story:** As a user, I want to log purchases against an envelope and see budget, spent, and left, so that I know how much flexible money remains.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user submits an Envelope, an amount, a purchase date, and an optional description, THE Entry_Service SHALL create a Line_Item assigned to that Envelope.
2. WHEN the user opens the add-purchase mode, THE Log_Flow SHALL pre-fill the purchase date with the Current_Date.
3. IF the purchase date is later than the Current_Date or is not a valid calendar date, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error describing the date rule, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
4. IF the submitted Envelope does not exist in the current Workspace, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with a not-found error, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
5. THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL compute Spent for an Envelope as the sum of the amounts of its Line_Items whose purchase dates fall within the Current_Period.
6. WHEN a Line_Item's purchase date falls in an Envelope_Period before the Current_Period, THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL count that Line_Item toward that earlier period and SHALL NOT include it in the Current_Period Spent.
7. THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL compute Left as the budget minus Spent, floored at 0, and Overage as Spent minus the budget, floored at 0.
8. THE Envelopes_Screen SHALL display, for each Envelope, its name, Reset_Period, Current_Period date range, budget, Spent, and Left.
9. THE Envelopes_Screen SHALL list the Line_Items of the Current_Period and of the immediately preceding Envelope_Period for each Envelope, each labeled with its period, with amount, purchase date, and description.
10. WHEN a Line_Item is created, THE Entry_Service SHALL decrease the ledger balance and the available balance of the Primary_Account by the Line_Item amount, in the same transaction, leaving a null available balance null.
11. FOR ALL Envelopes and sets of Line_Items, THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL satisfy Left minus Overage equal to the budget minus Spent, and at least one of Left and Overage SHALL equal 0 (invariant property).

### Requirement 9: Envelope Commitment in the Projection

**User Story:** As a user, I want the unspent part of each envelope counted as money already spoken for, so that my runway reflects spending I still plan to do.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an Envelope's Left is greater than 0, THE Runway_App SHALL supply the Projection_Engine with one Envelope_Commitment debit of amount Left, dated on the Commitment_Date.
2. WHEN an Envelope's Left is 0, THE Runway_App SHALL supply no Envelope_Commitment for that Envelope.
3. THE Projection_Engine SHALL apply an Envelope_Commitment on its Commitment_Date without business-day date shifting, reducing both the projected ledger balance and the projected available balance.
4. THE Runway_App SHALL NOT persist Envelope_Commitments, and SHALL recompute them from Envelope and Line_Item data on every projection request.
5. WHEN an Envelope is overspent, THE Runway_App SHALL show on the Envelopes_Screen the Overage amount and the amount by which the overspend lowered the runway, using a visually distinct overspent style that also includes a text label.
6. FOR ALL Envelopes with Left greater than 0, logging a Line_Item of amount X dated on the Current_Date where X is at most Left SHALL leave the runway lowest balance unchanged (invariant property).
7. FOR ALL Envelopes with Left equal to R, logging a Line_Item of amount X dated on the Current_Date where X is greater than R SHALL lower the runway lowest balance by exactly X minus R, provided the Commitment_Date and the Line_Item date are both on or before the runway date.
8. THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL compute Envelope_Commitments as a pure function with no I/O, and THE Projection_Engine SHALL remain free of database and network access.
9. WHEN the Projection_Engine receives no Envelope_Commitments and no Occurrence_Records, THE Projection_Engine SHALL produce output identical to its Spec 1 output for the same other inputs.

### Requirement 10: Edit and Delete Line Items

**User Story:** As a user, I want to fix or remove a purchase I logged, so that my envelope and runway stay correct.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user edits a Line_Item's amount, date, description, or Envelope, THE Entry_Service SHALL save the change and THE Runway_App SHALL recompute the affected Envelopes' Spent, Left, and Overage and the runway.
2. WHEN the user edits a Line_Item's amount from A to B, THE Entry_Service SHALL change the Primary_Account ledger and available balances by A minus B, in the same transaction, leaving a null available balance null.
3. WHEN the user deletes a Line_Item of amount A, THE Entry_Service SHALL increase the Primary_Account ledger and available balances by A, in the same transaction, leaving a null available balance null.
4. WHEN a Line_Item is moved to a purchase date in a different Envelope_Period, THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL count it toward the period containing the new date.
5. IF an edit submits an invalid amount, a future date, or an Envelope that does not exist in the current Workspace, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with a descriptive error, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
6. IF the user deletes a Line_Item that no longer exists, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL return a not-found error and SHALL NOT change any balance.
7. FOR ALL Line_Item sequences consisting of create followed by delete, THE Primary_Account balances SHALL equal their values before the create (round-trip property).

### Requirement 11: Double-Submit Protection

**User Story:** As a user on a slow connection, I want a repeated tap or retried request to be recorded only once, so that my balances and envelopes are never double-counted.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Log_Flow SHALL generate one Submission_Key when a form is opened and send that same key with every submission attempt of that form.
2. WHEN the Entry_Service receives a post, one-off, or purchase request whose Submission_Key was already recorded in the current Workspace, THE Entry_Service SHALL return the result of the original request and SHALL NOT create a second record or change any balance again.
3. WHEN the Entry_Service records a post, one-off, or purchase, THE Entry_Service SHALL store the Submission_Key in the same transaction as the change.
4. WHILE a submission is in progress, THE Log_Flow SHALL disable its submit control.
5. WHEN two requests with the same Submission_Key arrive concurrently, THE Entry_Service SHALL apply exactly one of them.
6. THE Runway_App SHALL include an automated test that submits the same post request twice and the same purchase request twice and verifies that each produces exactly one record and one balance change.
7. THE Entry_Service SHALL scope Submission_Keys to the Workspace, so that the same key used in two different Workspaces is applied in each.
8. WHEN the Entry_Service receives a one-off request with the Already_Happened_Option checked whose Submission_Key was already recorded in the current Workspace, THE Entry_Service SHALL return the result of the original request and SHALL NOT create a second Entry or change any balance again.
9. IF the Entry_Service rejects a one-off request, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL NOT record the request's Submission_Key.

### Requirement 12: Settings

**User Story:** As a user, I want to set my low-balance threshold and projection horizon, so that warnings and projections fit how I use my money.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Settings_Screen SHALL display the current Low_Balance_Threshold and Projection_Horizon of the Workspace in editable fields.
2. WHEN the user submits a Low_Balance_Threshold, THE Entry_Service SHALL validate it with the Money_Parser rules and the existing 0 to 999,999,999 cent range, and store it as integer cents.
3. WHEN the user clears the Low_Balance_Threshold field and submits, THE Entry_Service SHALL store no threshold (null), and THE Cycle_View SHALL omit the threshold warning unless the runway is negative.
4. WHEN the user submits a Projection_Horizon, THE Entry_Service SHALL accept an integer from 1 to 365 and store it.
5. IF the Projection_Horizon is not an integer from 1 to 365, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL reject the request with an error stating the allowed range, and SHALL NOT change any stored data.
6. WHEN a setting is saved, THE Cycle_View SHALL display the recomputed runway, warning state, and threshold marker without a manual page reload.
7. THE Settings_Screen SHALL leave schedule and account editing unavailable in this spec.

### Requirement 13: Runway Recomputation

**User Story:** As a user, I want the runway to update right after every change I make, so that I never look at stale numbers.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a post, skip, one-off creation, Envelope change, Line_Item creation, Line_Item edit, Line_Item deletion, or settings change succeeds, THE Runway_App SHALL request a fresh projection and update the Cycle_View and the Envelopes_Screen without a manual page reload.
2. WHEN the Runway_App requests a projection after a successful change, THE projection SHALL reflect that change.
3. IF an operation fails, THEN THE Log_Flow SHALL keep its form open with the entered values and display the error, and THE Runway_App SHALL show the previously stored values.
4. WHEN a mutation succeeds, THE Entry_Service SHALL update the Workspace's last-activity timestamp.

### Requirement 14: Envelopes Screen and Log Flow UI

**User Story:** As a user, I want an Envelopes screen and a fast Log flow that look and behave like the cycle view, so that logging takes a few seconds.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Envelopes_Screen SHALL render one card per Envelope using the same Card, Money, Chip, and PageHeader components and design tokens as the Cycle_View.
2. THE Envelopes_Screen SHALL display a progress indicator per Envelope that also exposes budget, Spent, and Left as text, so that meaning is not carried by color alone.
3. WHILE the Envelopes_Screen is loading data, THE Envelopes_Screen SHALL display a loading state.
4. IF the Workspace has no Envelopes, THEN THE Envelopes_Screen SHALL display an empty state with an action to create an Envelope.
5. IF the Envelopes_Screen fails to load data, THEN THE Envelopes_Screen SHALL display an error state with a Retry control, without crashing the application.
6. THE Log_Flow SHALL offer three modes: post an actual, add a purchase, and add a one-off.
7. THE Log_Flow SHALL place keyboard focus in the amount field when a mode opens, and SHALL submit when the user presses Enter in any field.
8. THE Log_Flow SHALL provide a programmatic label for each field and SHALL expose validation errors through an element with role "alert".
9. WHEN a Log_Flow submission succeeds, THE Log_Flow SHALL close and the updated runway SHALL be visible on the screen from which the Log_Flow was opened.
10. THE Log_Flow SHALL be reachable from both the Cycle_View and the Envelopes_Screen.
11. WHEN the user opens the post-actual mode from a timeline row on the Cycle_View, THE Log_Flow SHALL preselect that Entry.
12. THE Log_Flow SHALL provide a skip action for an Entry in the post-actual mode.
13. THE Log_Flow add-one-off mode SHALL include the Already_Happened_Option as a checkbox with the programmatic label "This already happened".
14. WHEN the date field of the add-one-off mode is today or earlier, THE Log_Flow SHALL show the Already_Happened_Option checked.
15. WHEN the date field of the add-one-off mode is later than the Current_Date, THE Log_Flow SHALL show the Already_Happened_Option unchecked.
16. WHEN the user changes the date field across the Current_Date boundary, THE Log_Flow SHALL reset the Already_Happened_Option to the default for the new date, unless the user has already toggled the Already_Happened_Option manually.
17. WHEN the add-one-off mode opens, THE Log_Flow SHALL pre-fill the date field with the Current_Date and show the Already_Happened_Option checked.
18. IF the Entry_Service rejects an add-one-off submission, THEN THE Log_Flow SHALL display the error message, including "Planned entries need a date today or later", through an element with role "alert".

### Requirement 15: Workspace Scoping and Reset

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want my edits kept private and restorable, so that I can experiment and return to the original demo.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Runway_App SHALL store `workspaceId` on every Envelope, Line_Item, Occurrence_Record, and Submission_Key, and every read and write SHALL filter by the current Workspace id.
2. IF a request references an Entry, Envelope, or Line_Item id that belongs to a different Workspace, THEN THE Entry_Service SHALL return a not-found error and SHALL NOT change any data in either Workspace.
3. WHEN a visitor triggers "Reset demo", THE Runway_App SHALL delete that Workspace's Envelopes, Line_Items, Occurrence_Records, and Submission_Keys, restore the Demo_Seed accounts, balances, entries, Low_Balance_Threshold, and Projection_Horizon, and leave every other Workspace unchanged.
4. WHEN "Reset demo" completes, THE Runway_App SHALL show the same runway, envelope totals, and settings as a newly created Workspace for the same Current_Date.
5. WHEN a Workspace is deleted by the 30-day inactivity cleanup, THE Runway_App SHALL delete its Envelopes, Line_Items, Occurrence_Records, and Submission_Keys.
6. THE Demo_Seed SHALL include at least three Envelopes covering EACH_PAY_CYCLE, WEEKLY, and MONTHLY, at least one Line_Item in the Current_Period, and at least one Line_Item in a preceding period.
7. THE Demo_Seed SHALL keep a computed runway below the Low_Balance_Threshold, preserving the Spec 1 demonstration of the low-balance warning.
8. THE Demo_Seed SHALL generate the Envelope and Line_Item dates relative to the Current_Date.

### Requirement 16: Regression Safety

**User Story:** As a developer, I want Spec 1 behavior preserved, so that adding actuals and envelopes does not break the projection.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Runway_App SHALL pass every Spec 1 automated test without modification to its assertions.
2. THE Projection_Engine SHALL remain pure and SHALL perform no I/O after this spec's changes.
3. THE Envelope_Calculator SHALL reside in `src/lib/envelopes/` and the Money_Parser SHALL reside under `src/lib/`, and neither SHALL import database, network, or source modules.
4. THE Runway_App SHALL continue to read account data through the AccountDataSource interface.
5. THE Envelope_Calculator and the Money_Parser SHALL have Vitest unit tests, including property-based tests for the properties stated in Requirements 1, 6, 8, 9, and 10.
6. THE Runway_App SHALL leave the Spec 1 definition of Overdue_Entry unchanged for PLANNED Entries, so that every Spec 1 overdue test continues to pass.
